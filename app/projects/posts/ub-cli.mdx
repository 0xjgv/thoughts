All credit goes to [Robert C. Martin (Uncle Bob)](https://github.com/unclebob). He built [ub-mode](https://github.com/unclebob/ub-mode) and its three engines:

- [Crapper](https://github.com/unclebob/crapper) measures CRAP scores.
- [Dryer](https://github.com/unclebob/dryer) detects duplicates.
- [Mutator](https://github.com/unclebob/mutator) runs mutation testing.

ub-cli packages these tools as one installable `ub` command with a combined `ub check` gate.
